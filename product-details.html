PHP Arabian Horse Super NOF Dhew Tin

PHP Arabian Horse Super is mostly trusted because it maintains perfect weight, accurate size, and consistent thickness in every Dhew Tin; something many brands fail to deliver.

PHP Arabian Horse Super NOF Dhew Tin is a premium grade corrugated galvanized iron (CGI) sheet manufactured using advanced Non-Oxidizing Furnace (NOF) technology a process that ensures exceptional coating uniformity, superior rust protection, and longer lifespan. In Bangladesh, it stands as one of the most trusted and durable roofing solutions under the PHP Family Steel Division.v

Key Features :

  • High-Quality Galvanized Steel — strong and rust-resistant
  • NOF Coating Process – smooth finish & uniform zinc layer
  • Chromic Treatment – added shine & durability
  • Wavy (Dhew) Design – better strength & water drainage
  • Lightweight & Easy to Install
  • Weather-Resistant – ideal for Bangladesh climate

Key Advantages :

  • Superior Durability: Long-lasting protection in all weather conditions
  • Corrosion Resistant: Dual-sided zinc coating prevents rust even in coastal climates
  • Easy to Install: Saves both labor and installation time
  • Enhanced Water Drainage: The wavy corrugation allows smooth water flow
  • Economical: Excellent cost-to-performance ratio with low maintenance needs
  • Aesthetic Appeal: Available in both galvanized and color-coated finishes for modern structures

Applications :

  • Residential Roofing: Houses, garages, boundary shades
  • Industrial Buildings: Factories, warehouses, workshops
  • Agricultural Projects: Poultry farms, storage sheds, rural housing
  • Commercial Infrastructure: Markets, school buildings, community centers

Thickness 0.120 mm – 0.510 mm
Width 28 to 32 inches
Length 6 ft – 20 ft / custom
